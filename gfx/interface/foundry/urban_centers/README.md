# Foundry Urban Center Density Artwork

Nine panoramic stages showing one British settlement developing across the
1760–1960 campaign. Runtime images are `380x100`; `_master.png` files retain
the generated full-resolution artwork.

All stages use the same elevated viewpoint, terrain, church, main roads,
muted painted palette, and Victoria 2-inspired presentation. The stage prompts
advance the scene as follows:

0. Circa 1760: undeveloped countryside at the future settlement site.
1. Circa 1760: rural village, church, cottages, dirt lanes, dominant farmland.
2. Circa 1785: growing market town, workshops, square, improved roads.
3. Circa 1815: early industrial town, Georgian streets, textile mills.
4. Circa 1845: railway-era city, brick terraces, station, factories.
5. Circa 1875: mature industrial city, rail yards, civic buildings, gaslight.
6. Circa 1900: electrified metropolis, trams, power and water infrastructure.
7. Circa 1930: interwar city, buses, early automobiles, Art Deco civic growth.
8. Circa 1960: mature modern city, commuter rail, restrained modernism.

Shared negative prompt: no UI, frames, flags, text, labels, symbols,
watermarks, futuristic architecture, or visually dominant modern highways.
